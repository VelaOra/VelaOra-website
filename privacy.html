Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  VelaOra, New Zealand

VelaOra is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how we use it — in plain language. It is written in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

Who We Are

VelaOra is a New Zealand–based women's health platform providing educational content, tools, and wellness resources. We operate at velaora.co.nz and can be contacted at velaorawomen@gmail.com.

What Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our services:

  • Email address and name — when you subscribe to VelaOra, submit a contact form, or sign up for a paid product such as Restora.
  • Website usage data — anonymous analytics data collected via Google Analytics (pages visited, time on site, device type). This does not identify you personally.
  • Form submissions — messages sent through our contact and subscription forms are processed by Formspree, our third-party form provider.
  • Payment information — if you purchase a VelaOra product, payment is processed by our third-party payment provider. We do not store your card details on our servers.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as health data, ethnicity, or government identifiers) unless you voluntarily share it with us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you the VelaOra newsletter and articles you have subscribed to.
  • To provide access to paid products or services you have purchased.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via our contact form.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve our content (via anonymous analytics).
  • To comply with our legal obligations under New Zealand law.

We will not use your information for any purpose other than those listed above without your explicit consent.

Who We Share Your Information With

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate VelaOra:

  • Formspree — processes contact and subscription form submissions. Formspree Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous website usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Mailchimp / email platform — used to send newsletters and updates. Your email address is stored on their servers in accordance with their privacy policy.
  • Payment processor — if applicable, your payment details are handled by our payment provider and subject to their privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes — ever.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your email address and subscription data for as long as you remain subscribed to VelaOra communications. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or by contacting us directly. We will remove your data within 30 days of your request.

Cookies

VelaOra uses cookies to support Google Analytics and basic website function. These are non-identifying and do not track you across other websites. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Your Rights Under the NZ Privacy Act 2020

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to any legal obligations we have to retain it).
  • Withdraw consent to us using your information at any time.
  • Make a complaint to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz if you believe we have mishandled your information.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at velaorawomen@gmail.com. We will respond within 20 working days.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Our website is served over HTTPS. Third-party services we use maintain their own security standards. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

Children

VelaOra is designed for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, please contact us at velaorawomen@gmail.com or via the contact form on our website.

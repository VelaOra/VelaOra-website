Menopause Skincare

The Menopause Face Cream I Now Buy Myself (and Why It Became My Morning Ritual)

Honesty first: after a friend recommended this cream, I asked Antipodes for a sample to review, and they sent one. Every tub since, and the other Antipodes products I now use, I've bought with my own money. Nobody has paid me to write this, and there are no affiliate links in this post.

The skincare that works best for me right now isn't the one with the longest ingredient list or the biggest promises. It's the one that makes me feel something when I use it.

Somewhere in perimenopause my skin quietly changed the rules. Drier, more reactive, a bit less bounce. My old routine started to feel like it was working against me instead of for me.

So when I find something that genuinely makes my skin feel good, and makes me feel good using it, I want to tell you about it. This is one of those.

The cream that turned into a ritual

A friend recommended Antipodes Jasmine Flower Menopause Facial Cream, so I asked Antipodes for a sample to try. I'll be honest: when I first opened the pot, I wasn't sure about the scent. I very nearly didn't get past that first impression.

I'm so glad I did. Somewhere over those first few weeks, the smell went from "hmm" to the best part of my morning. It's almost like aromatherapy. Those couple of minutes massaging it in have become a small moment that's just for me. It genuinely makes me feel more awake and more alive at the start of the day.

The texture is lovely too. On my skin it feels rich and nourishing without sitting heavily, and my face feels comfortable and hydrated rather than tight.

The real test? I finished that sample, missed the smell, and bought my own. I've since bought more of the Antipodes range too. Honestly, they all smell so good. For me, paying for something myself after trying it for free is the only review that really counts.

What's Actually In It

Antipodes is a New Zealand brand from Wellington, and this cream is made for skin from perimenopause through to post-menopause. The key ingredients Antipodes highlights are organic oat extract to calm redness and irritation, jasmine flower peptides aimed at smoothing lines and evening skin tone, and mānuka honey for hydration.

The fragrance comes from plants (COSMOS-certified jasmine), and Antipodes says it's been dermatologically approved for sensitive skin. It's around NZ$70 for 60ml at full price. Antipodes recommends using it in the morning after a serum. As always, patch test first if your skin is reactive.

I've also started the serum from the same range. I'm giving it a fair trial before I tell you what I think.

The little green tube I keep coming back to

The other product I've really loved lately is Weleda Skin Food, the small tube. It's a great size for travel, and you honestly only need a tiny amount. It leaves my skin looking glowy and feeling properly hydrated.

Good to know: Skin Food has been made since 1926. It's a rich cream of plant oils, beeswax and lanolin with pansy, chamomile, calendula and rosemary extracts. It's made for very dry, rough skin, on your face or anywhere else that needs it. It's thick, so start with less than you think you need.

Here's what I really want you to hear

Our skin changing isn't a failure. It's not something to fight or hide. It's biology, and it's completely normal.

You don't need to chase the skin you had at thirty. You deserve products that meet your skin where it is now, and a few minutes each day that feel like care rather than correction.

If that's a cream that smells like a little moment of calm, that's not vanity. That's looking after yourself.

— Sarah, VelaOra
